# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Investors and partners weighing whether to back or work with Emmanuel Charles's companies; customers of those companies (Kenyan businesses and consumers) who should be routed to the right product; press, prospective hires and the wider East African tech ecosystem trying to understand who he is and what he is building. Most arrive on a phone over mobile data. Occasional enterprise or development inquiries are a side door, not the headline.

## Product Purpose

A founder's site for Emmanuel Charles, not a freelancer's portfolio. It presents the companies he has built and runs as one body of work: Munchify (food delivery marketplace, 20,000+ orders a week), Cyzora (M-Pesa payment collection and payouts for Kenyan businesses) and Zyra Net (fast WiFi internet in Kisumu). Success is a visitor who leaves understanding that one founder is building durable infrastructure for daily life in Kenya, and who knows exactly where to go next: the company they need, or a direct line to him.

## Brand Personality

Weight, permanence, legacy. Calm authority, long-term thinking, plainspoken. Three words: grounded, durable, exacting. Voice is first person, specific and understated: facts and consequences over adjectives, no hustle language.

## Anti-references

- Freelancer or agency portfolios: project grids, "hire me", pricing tiers, process steps, client testimonials, "let's build together".
- Generic SaaS templates: gradient hero, hero-metric stat row, identical icon cards, a small uppercase eyebrow above every section.
- Startup-bro hype: "disrupting", hustle tone, bragging numbers without context.
- Corporate stiffness: stock-photo boardrooms, cold navy-and-gold conglomerate look.
- Gimmicks that fight usability: custom cursors, heavy smooth-scroll hijacking.

## Design Principles

1. **Companies, not deliverables.** Every claim is about something he founded and still runs. Nothing on the page describes work done for a client.
2. **Legacy through specifics.** Permanence is shown with real facts (what each company does, its scale, where it operates), never with adjectives or vague superlatives.
3. **Only sourced claims.** State only what appears on each company's own site or what the founder has supplied. No invented metrics, dates or testimonials.
4. **One founder, several doors.** Each visitor type (investor, customer, press) finds their route in one step; company links go to the live products.
5. **Light on the phone.** Built for Kenyan mobile connections: lean images and JS, fast first paint, motion that never gates content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus, full keyboard operation. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Mobile-first and low-bandwidth: optimised images, minimal client JS. No custom cursor; no scroll hijacking.
